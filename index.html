Welcome to My Weather App.

Professional weather reporting tool for Observers, Meteorologists and Aviation Professionals.

Welcome to the Weather Report Generator, a specialized tool designed for Observers, Meteorologists, Aviation Professionals, and Weather Enthusiasts. This platform simplifies the creation of SYNOPs, ACTUALS, FLIGHT FOLDER, DUTY ROSTER, and METAR/SPECI reports with standardized formatting.

Key Features:

  • METAR/SPECI reports compliant with ICAO/WMO standards
  • Form fields for SYNOP and ACTUALS data with auto-validation
  • Save, export, and email reports directly from the platform
  • Mobile-responsive design for on-the-go use
